Questions to resolve before signing

This tool does not verify a contractor

Confirm licensing requirements and status with the relevant state or local agency, request current insurance evidence, check references and complaint history, read the full contract, and understand local cancellation and deposit rules. Requirements vary by location.

How to compare written estimates

The Federal Trade Commission recommends getting multiple written estimates and checking that they describe the work, materials, completion timing, and price. A major price difference deserves an explanation; it may reflect a different scope, material allowance, crew, permit assumption, warranty, or exclusion.

Do not automatically select the lowest price. Compare the underlying work, verify credentials where required, and avoid pressure to make an immediate decision or pay the full project price upfront. State rules about deposits, licensing, cancellation, and contract language vary.
