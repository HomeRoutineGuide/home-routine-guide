A calmer way to keep up

Small routines prevent big surprises.

You do not need to become a contractor. You need a reliable system that helps you notice changes, remember recurring tasks, and know when professional help makes sense.

  • ✓ Prioritized by urgency, season, and effort
  • ✓ Plain-language steps with realistic time estimates
  • ✓ Clear DIY boundaries and professional-call guidance
  • ✓ Product suggestions tied to real maintenance tasks

Spring routine

Inspect, refresh, and catch winter damage.

  • Walk the exterior and look for visible changes
  • Check gutters and drainage paths
  • Prepare cooling equipment for warmer weather
  • Review outdoor faucets, screens, and seals
