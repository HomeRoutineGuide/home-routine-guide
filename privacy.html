Privacy policy

A plain-language description of the site’s current data practices during its initial launch phase.

Effective July 24, 2026

Current launch-stage practices

Home Routine Guide currently provides public webpages and a browser-saved checklist. The site does not currently offer user accounts, process payments, operate an email signup form, or intentionally collect sensitive home records.

Browser-saved checklist data

The interactive First 30 Days checklist uses your browser’s local storage to remember completed items. That information stays in the browser on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to Home Routine Guide. You can remove it by using the checklist reset button or clearing browser site data.

Hosting and routine technical data

The site is currently hosted through GitHub Pages. Hosting providers and internet infrastructure may process routine technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time, referring page, and error or security logs as part of delivering and protecting the service. Their processing is governed by their own policies.

Fonts and external links

The site currently requests fonts from Google Fonts. External links may take you to government agencies, manufacturers, retailers, or other websites. Those services may collect information under their own privacy policies. Home Routine Guide does not control third-party sites.

Analytics

Home Routine Guide does not currently run a dedicated analytics or advertising tracker. Before adding analytics, advertising, affiliate tracking, or similar technologies, this policy will be updated and any legally required notices or choices will be implemented.

Email

A business email and mailing list are being configured. When email signup becomes available, the page will explain what information is collected, why it is collected, which service provider processes it, and how to unsubscribe.

Payments and digital products

No payments are currently processed on the site. Before paid products launch, this policy will identify the payment and delivery providers used and describe the categories of information involved.

Home-related information

Future tools may let users organize home systems, maintenance history, or documents. We intend to minimize collection and favor on-device storage where practical. No future feature should be assumed to be private or secure until its specific privacy explanation is published.

Children

The site is intended for adults managing or purchasing a home. It is not directed to children.

Security

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Home Routine Guide will use reasonable measures appropriate to the services offered and will avoid collecting information that is unnecessary for the task.

Policy changes

This policy will change as email, analytics, products, accounts, or other features are introduced. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date.

Launch limitation

A public contact address and privacy-request process will be added after the new business email is fully configured.
