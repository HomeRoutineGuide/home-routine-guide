Product questions

What to expect from the 44-page digital binder.

Are the forms generic?

No. The enhanced edition uses prompts, examples, “what to enter” guidance, and safer recordkeeping reminders so you know what useful information belongs on the page.

Is the Starter Binder available today?

Yes. The enhanced 44-page New Homeowner Starter Binder is available as a one-time $19 digital purchase. The larger systems are still upcoming.

Will the free guides disappear?

No. The free resources remain the public educational library. The paid binder adds reusable guided worksheets and organization.

Will this replace an inspector or contractor?

No. The binder is educational and organizational. It cannot evaluate a property, diagnose a defect, or replace qualified professional advice.

Is there a subscription?

No subscription is required. It is a one-time digital purchase delivered through Kit.

Can I print pages again?

Yes. Keep the original PDF so you can print replacement working pages for your own household as needed, subject to the digital product terms.
