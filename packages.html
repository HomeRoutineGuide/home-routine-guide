Questions before launch

What to expect from the first release.

Are the packages available today?

No. The files, checkout, delivery process, and customer terms are still being completed. The newsletter will announce the release.

Will the free guides disappear?

No. The free resources are the public educational library. Paid packages add organization, templates, and reusable planning systems.

Will this replace an inspector or contractor?

No. The packages are educational and organizational. They cannot evaluate a property, diagnose a defect, or replace local professional advice.

Will there be a subscription?

The initial products are planned as one-time digital purchases. A separate optional dashboard may be considered later.
