Product questions

What to expect from the digital binder.

Is the Starter Binder available today?

Yes. The New Homeowner Starter Binder is available as a one-time $19 digital purchase. The First-Year System and Master Binder are still upcoming.

Will the free guides disappear?

No. The free resources remain the public educational library. The paid binder adds organization, templates, and reusable planning pages.

Will this replace an inspector or contractor?

No. The binder is educational and organizational. It cannot evaluate a property, diagnose a defect, or replace local professional advice.

Is there a subscription?

No subscription is required for the Starter Binder. It is sold as a one-time digital purchase through Kit.
