Why guided forms matter

Generic blank sheets create another problem: deciding what belongs on them.

The enhanced binder is written for a homeowner who may not already know which equipment details, repair notes, contractor information, or maintenance decisions will matter later.

Record locations precisely

Prompts encourage useful locations such as “basement utility room, north wall” instead of vague notes like “basement.”

Separate observation from diagnosis

Repair and moisture pages distinguish what you noticed from a confirmed professional diagnosis, work performed, and final outcome.

Always capture the next action

Service and planning forms include follow-up dates, invoice references, warranty information, monitoring decisions, and next steps.
