Trust and transparency

Editorial policy

The standards we use to research, write, review, update, correct, and monetize Home Routine Guide content.

Effective July 24, 2026

Our editorial purpose

Home Routine Guide exists to help homeowners organize decisions and understand routine property care. Our content is educational and practical. It is not a substitute for an on-site evaluation, local code requirements, manufacturer instructions, licensed trade advice, or emergency services.

Source hierarchy

We prefer sources in roughly this order:

  1. Federal, state, and local government agencies
  2. Current manufacturer instructions and technical documentation
  3. Recognized standards and safety organizations
  4. Licensed or credentialed professional organizations
  5. Original interviews, expert review, and documented testing
  6. High-quality secondary reporting when a primary source is unavailable

We do not treat retailer descriptions, affiliate-network copy, anonymous forum posts, or AI-generated summaries as sufficient evidence for safety or performance claims.

How articles are prepared

Each substantive guide should identify the reader’s practical question, define the limits of the page, research relevant primary sources, separate observation from diagnosis, and explain when professional help may be appropriate. We aim to use plain language without removing important safety context.

Use of artificial intelligence

AI tools may assist with outlining, editing, code, organization, and research discovery. AI output is not treated as a source. Factual claims intended for publication should be checked against reliable sources, and safety-sensitive instructions should receive additional scrutiny before publication.

Professional review

As the publication grows, pages involving electrical, structural, plumbing, fuel-burning, fire-safety, environmental, legal, insurance, or financial topics may be reviewed by appropriately qualified professionals. When professional review occurs, the reviewer’s role and review date should be disclosed on the page.

Product recommendations

We evaluate products in the context of a real homeowner task. Recommendations should consider safety, compatibility, instructions, durability, usability, cost, warranty, and whether a purchase is necessary at all. We will not claim hands-on testing unless we actually performed and documented it.

Payment, free products, sponsorships, or affiliate commissions do not guarantee favorable coverage. Material commercial relationships will be disclosed clearly.

Updates and review dates

Important guides display a reviewed or updated date. A date means the page was checked for material accuracy and relevance at that time; it does not guarantee that every external source or local rule remains unchanged. Readers should verify current local requirements and manufacturer documentation.

Corrections

Material factual errors should be corrected promptly once verified. Significant corrections may be noted on the affected page. Minor spelling, formatting, or clarity edits may be made without a correction note.

Commercial separation

Editorial decisions are made for reader usefulness. Sponsored content, paid placements, and affiliate relationships will be labeled. Advertisers and merchants will not receive the right to secretly rewrite independent editorial conclusions.

Reader responsibility

Every property is different. Readers are responsible for deciding whether they have the skill, permission, equipment, and safe conditions to perform a task. Stop when conditions are uncertain, hazardous, or beyond your competence.

Our operating rule

Helpful enough to act on, honest enough to show the limits, and calm enough to avoid fear-based selling.
