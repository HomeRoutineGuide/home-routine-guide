# Home Routine Guide

**Know what your home needs next.**

Home Routine Guide is a practical homeowner education platform for first-time and still-figuring-it-out homeowners. It combines calm, sourced guidance with browser-based tools, printable systems, and transparent monetization.

## Editorial principles

1. Use primary sources whenever practical.
2. Separate observation and organization from diagnosis and repair instruction.
3. Explain DIY limits and when qualified help is appropriate.
4. Do not claim hands-on product testing unless it occurred.
5. Disclose affiliate, sponsorship, advertising, and other material relationships clearly.
6. Do not create artificial urgency or imply every homeowner needs every product.
7. Prefer a maintained, useful guide over high-volume shallow content.

## Monetization roadmap

### Phase 1 — audience and trust

- Free First 30 Days printable
- Email newsletter and seasonal reminders
- Search-focused cornerstone guides
- Useful browser tools with no login

### Phase 2 — first product

- Focus the offer on the $19, 44-page New Homeowner Starter Binder
- Use the free checklist and buyer-intent guides to lead into one clear paid product
- Improve the binder and sales page from real subscriber and customer questions before adding another offer

### Phase 3 — careful affiliate revenue

- Task-specific product guides
- Clear page-level affiliate disclosures
- Manufacturer and safety verification
- Original comparisons and, where possible, documented testing

### Phase 4 — recurring product

- Optional homeowner dashboard
- Personalized maintenance schedule
- Equipment and service records
- Replacement-planning and reminder tools
